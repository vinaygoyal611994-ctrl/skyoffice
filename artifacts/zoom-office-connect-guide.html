Zoom Integration — Office Account Connection

How an office connects its own Zoom account to enable automatic video call meetings

Date: November 2025 Scope: Office dashboard → Zoom Integration section Role: Office user only

Overview

Each office on SkayOffice can connect its own Zoom account using OAuth 2.0. Once connected, whenever the office accepts a video call booking request from a customer, a Zoom meeting is automatically created. The customer receives the join link and the office receives the host link — no manual scheduling needed.

Multi-tenancy

Every office connects its own independent Zoom account. One office's Zoom credentials are never shared with or visible to any other office.

How to Connect Your Zoom Account

  1. Log in to your office account Go to the SkayOffice site and sign in with your office credentials.
  2. Open the Dashboard After login you will land on the office dashboard automatically.
  3. Click "Zoom Integration" in the sidebar Find the Zoom Integration item in the left navigation menu of the dashboard and click it.
  4. Click "Connect Zoom" On the Zoom Integration page, click the blue Connect Zoom button.
  5. Authorize on Zoom's website You will be redirected to Zoom's official authorization page. Log in with your Zoom account and click Allow to grant SkayOffice the required permissions.
  6. Return to dashboard — connection confirmed Zoom redirects you back to SkayOffice. A success message appears and the status badge changes to Connected. Your Zoom email address is shown on the page.

Done — Zoom is now active

From this point, every accepted video call booking will automatically create a Zoom meeting. No further setup is required.

Connection Status Indicators

The Zoom Integration page shows the current status at all times:

Not Connected Connected Refreshing… Re-auth Required Disconnected Error
Status Meaning Action Required
Not Connected No Zoom account has been linked yet Click "Connect Zoom"
Connected Zoom account is linked and working None — meetings create automatically
Refreshing… Access token is being renewed in the background Wait a moment, then refresh the page
Re-auth Required Zoom revoked access (e.g. password change, app removed) Click "Reconnect Zoom" to re-authorize
Disconnected Office manually disconnected Zoom Click "Connect Zoom" to reconnect
Error Unexpected error fetching status Refresh the page; reconnect if issue persists

Reconnect or Disconnect

Reconnect

If you want to switch to a different Zoom account, or if the status shows Re-auth Required, click Reconnect Zoom. You will go through the OAuth flow again and the new account will replace the old one.

Disconnect

Click Disconnect Zoom (red button) to unlink your Zoom account. Existing booking records and past meeting links are preserved — only future accepted bookings will no longer auto-create a meeting.

Permissions Requested from Zoom

SkayOffice requests only the minimum permissions needed:

  • View your Zoom profile (to show connected email)
  • Create meetings (to auto-create on booking accept)
  • View meetings (to verify meeting status)
  • Delete meetings (to cancel if booking is declined)

Recording, webinar, chat, and participant management permissions are never requested.

Security

Zoom access and refresh tokens are never stored in plaintext. They are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before being saved to the database. Tokens are automatically refreshed in the background before expiry — the office does not need to reconnect periodically under normal conditions.
