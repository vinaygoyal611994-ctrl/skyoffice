Perform a comprehensive, read-only technical audit of the current SkayOffice production codebase only.

Scope:
- Production version only.
- Do not inspect, modify, clean, refactor, delete, rename, format, optimize, migrate, or update anything.
- Do not touch development, staging, test environments, production data, uploaded files, environment variables, database records, routes, APIs, dependencies, or configuration.
- This task is inspection and reporting only.

Before starting:
1. Confirm the exact production codebase and production environment being inspected.
2. Confirm that no changes will be made.
3. Record the current production build/version/commit if available.
4. Do not create automatic fixes or apply recommendations.
