Final product requirements

1. Quick Display Card
- Exactly ONE independent image.
- This image belongs only to the Quick Display Card.
- It must not be reused as the Decision Card image fallback.
- The office owner can upload, replace, or delete it.
- Replacing or deleting it must not affect any Decision Card media.

2. Decision Card images
- Exactly FOUR image slots total.
- Do not expose separate “Decision Card Cover” and “Decision Gallery” concepts to the user.
- The four images form one unified ordered carousel.
- Position 1 is the first image displayed when the Decision Card opens.
- The office owner must be able to:
  - upload into an empty slot,
  - replace one specific image,
  - delete one specific image,
  - reorder all four images.
- Replacing, deleting, approving, or rejecting one image must never alter the other three images.
- Maximum: 4 images.
- Minimum: 1 approved image for public display.
- If all four slots are full, show a bilingual message explaining that the maximum has been reached and that an existing image must be replaced or deleted.

3. Approval behavior for active offices
- Each new or replacement image must go through admin approval independently.
- The currently approved image must remain publicly visible until its replacement is approved.
- Approval of one slot must affect only that slot.
- Rejection must preserve the currently approved version.
- No sibling image may be silently rejected, hidden, or deleted.
- Pending, approved, rejected, and replacement states must be clear in the office dashboard and admin dashboard.

4. Decision Card carousel
- Auto-rotate approved images every exactly 8 seconds.
- Activate only when there are at least 2 approved images.
- Support:
  - mobile swipe,
  - dot indicators,
  - RTL and LTR,
  - infinite loop,
  - pause on touch,
  - pause on hover,
  - pause while video is playing,
  - safe resume after user interaction,
  - reduced-motion preference.
- Manual navigation must not break the timer.
- No stretched or distorted images.

5. Decision Card image size
- Change the carousel and loading skeleton ratio from:
  - aspect-[8/3]
  to:
  - aspect-[7/3]
- Confirm the change on:
  - 320px mobile
  - 390px mobile
  - desktop/max-width card
- Ensure the WhatsApp and Request Service buttons are not pushed too far down.

6. Optional marketing video
- Exactly ONE optional video for the Decision Card.
- Maximum duration: 60 seconds.
- It must never autoplay.
- Display a clear play button.
- The visitor chooses whether to play it.
- Support fullscreen.
- Pause image auto-rotation while the video is playing.
- Resume safely after the video is paused or closed.
- The office owner can upload, replace, or delete the video.
- Video changes for active offices must go through approval without removing the currently approved video before the replacement is approved.
- Validate duration client-side and server-side.
- Reject files longer than 60 seconds with clear Arabic and English messages.

7. Request Service button
