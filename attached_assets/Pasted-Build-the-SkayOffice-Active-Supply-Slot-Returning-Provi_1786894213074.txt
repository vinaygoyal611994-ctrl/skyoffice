Build the SkayOffice Active Supply Slot + Returning Provider Waitlist system.

This must be completed BEFORE payment gateway integration.

Do NOT change Market Decision Intelligence rules.
Do NOT build payment integration yet.
Do NOT delete or reset any office history.

Core principle

Separate:

- Account
- Subscription
- Supply Slot
- Public Visibility

An office may have an active subscription but must NOT automatically become publicly visible if its Country + City + Category market has no available Supply Slot.

Rule:

Account is permanent.
Reputation/history are permanent.
Subscription can renew.
Visibility requires an allocated market slot.

1. Slot status

Add a clear slot lifecycle, for example:

- allocated
- waiting
- released
- not_required

Document the final states.

"current_active_supply" must represent providers that actually occupy visible marketplace slots — not all historical accounts and not all subscriptions.

2. Expired/non-paying office

When an office becomes hidden due to non-payment:

- preserve account
- preserve subscription history
- preserve reviews
- preserve transactions
- preserve reputation
- preserve profile/media
- release its active Supply Slot

Do not delete anything.

3. Returning office

When a previously hidden office renews/reactivates:

If capacity exists

Allocate a slot and restore visibility.

If market is full

Do NOT make it provider #16 in a cap of 15.

Instead:

- subscription may become active
- slot_status = waiting
- office remains hidden from public discovery
- office enters the market waitlist

4. Waitlist priority

Returning previously-active providers should have priority over brand-new providers, but they must NEVER bypass Supply Cap.

Create a simple deterministic priority model.

At minimum:

1. eligible returning provider
2. other existing eligible applications
3. new provider

Do not build a complex AI ranking system.

5. Automatic slot release and allocation

When capacity becomes available because:

- a provider expires
- a provider cancels
- a provider leaves
- Admin increases Supply Cap

the system should identify available slots and allocate them safely according to waitlist priority.

Do not exceed Supply Cap.

Admin must be able to see what was allocated and why.

6. Subscription interaction

Important:

"active_paid" does NOT necessarily mean "visible".

Example:

subscription_status = active_paid
slot_status = waiting
visibility = hidden_waiting_for_slot

Only:

subscription active + slot allocated

may restore public visibility.

Update existing reactivation logic so it cannot blindly clear "is_subscription_hidden" when the market is full.

7. Supply Cap integration

Use the existing:

Country + Canonical City + Category + Supply Cap

logic.

Closed markets remain closed.

Cap enforcement must remain transaction-safe.

Concurrent reactivations must not allocate the same final slot twice.

8. Admin UI

Inside Supply Control / Subscription detail, show:

- Current Supply Cap
- Active Slots
- Remaining Slots
- Waiting Providers
- Returning Providers Waiting
- Slot status per office

Allow Admin to inspect the waitlist and perform an audited exceptional override if required.

Do not clutter the main dashboard.

9. Office-facing status

Prepare a simple office status message:

If subscription is active but no slot is available:

Your subscription/profile is preserved. Your market is currently at capacity and your office is waiting for the next available visibility slot.

Provide Arabic + English.

Do not charge or promise payment behavior yet — payment gateway is not integrated.

10. Audit history

Record:

- slot allocated
- slot released
- waitlist entered
- waitlist exited
- return priority
- admin override
- reason
- market
- timestamps

Never overwrite history.

11. Tests

Test at minimum:

- cap 15 with 15 active slots → returning office cannot become #16
- expired office releases slot
- returning office gets free slot when available
- returning office waits when full
- returning provider priority beats new provider
- priority never bypasses cap
- cap increase allocates newly available slots correctly
- cap reduction never removes existing providers
- two concurrent returning providers cannot claim one slot
- active subscription can exist while slot_status = waiting
- waiting office remains publicly hidden
- allocated office becomes visible
- history/reviews/transactions remain intact
- SA/AE and city/category isolation remain correct
- full existing test suite passes

Return:

1. data model
2. slot lifecycle
3. exact slot-consumer definition
4. release logic
5. waitlist logic
6. returning-provider priority
7. subscription/visibility interaction
8. concurrency protection
