---
name: SkayOffice media placement isolation
description: Durable rules for keeping optional Works media independent from Decision Card marketing media.
---

Treat Works as an explicit media role. Never infer it from, alias it to, or reclassify legacy Decision Card gallery media. Public Works visibility is content-driven from approved Works only; pending Works remains owner/admin-only.

**Why:** Decision Card marketing images and portfolio samples have different customer-facing purposes. Reusing one collection for both caused marketing media to appear as claimed work and made ordering changes affect the wrong surface.

**How to apply:** Every Works upload, reorder, delete, and moderation request must carry or preserve the Works placement. Enforce its four non-rejected-item cap and ordering under one per-office transaction lock, and keep its image processing and lightbox collection independent.
